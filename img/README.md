# Build photos

Drop your own photos here and reference them from `index.html` as
`<img src="img/your-file.jpg" alt="what it shows">`.

Naming that stays findable a year from now:

    <section>-<subject>-<view>.jpg
    5.3-d44-hub-taper-before.jpg
    5.1-bodymount-3-shims.jpg
    5.4-perch-location-driver.jpg

Only your own photographs belong here. Diagrams from vendor catalogs and
the CTS-2302 manual are copyrighted — section 10 of the plan links to them
at the source instead of copying them into this repo, which is public.
